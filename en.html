Welcome

Welcome to our bright and stylish holiday apartment in the heart of Porto Cristo. The apartment is located on the first floor of a well-maintained two-family house in a quiet side street, combining a central location with a pleasant and relaxing atmosphere.

The sandy beach, picturesque fishing harbour, restaurants, cafés and shops are all within just a few minutes' walk. There are usually plenty of free public parking spaces available directly in front of the house.

The spacious apartment features two bedrooms, two modern bathrooms – one of them en suite – as well as a bright and comfortable living room.

The separate kitchen is fully equipped. A utility room with a washing machine is also available.

One of the highlights is the large private sun terrace. The outdoor area is complemented by a balcony with a seating area.

Several air-conditioning units with cooling and heating functions ensure pleasant temperatures all year round. Large built-in wardrobes provide plenty of storage space. The apartment is ideal for up to four people and, upon request, also suitable for a small pet.
